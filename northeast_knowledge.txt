NER-SMARTLOG KNOWLEDGE BASE

PROJECT
NER-SMARTLOG is a smart logistics and disaster-awareness system focused on
Northeast India. The system helps users understand transportation,
logistics, weather awareness, routes, and disaster-related risks.

REGION
NER-SMARTLOG covers the eight states of Northeast India:

1. Assam
2. Arunachal Pradesh
3. Meghalaya
4. Manipur
5. Mizoram
6. Nagaland
7. Tripura
8. Sikkim

ASSAM
Capital: Dispur
Major city: Guwahati

Logistics considerations:
- Guwahati is an important transportation and logistics hub for Northeast India.
- Major roads and highways connect Assam with other northeastern states.
- Heavy rainfall and flooding can affect transportation.
- Road conditions should be checked before long-distance logistics operations.

ARUNACHAL PRADESH
Capital: Itanagar

Logistics considerations:
- Mountainous terrain can make transportation challenging.
- Heavy rainfall can increase the risk of landslides.
- Road conditions can change rapidly in mountainous areas.
- Logistics planning should include alternative routes where possible.

MEGHALAYA
Capital: Shillong

Logistics considerations:
- Meghalaya has mountainous terrain and receives significant rainfall.
- Heavy rainfall can create landslide and road-disruption risks.
- Logistics operators should consider weather conditions before travelling through vulnerable areas.

MANIPUR
Capital: Imphal

Logistics considerations:
- Imphal is an important logistics and transportation centre.
- Mountainous terrain and weather conditions can affect transportation.
- Route conditions should be verified before making operational decisions.

MIZORAM
Capital: Aizawl

Logistics considerations:
- Mizoram has predominantly hilly terrain.
- Heavy rainfall can increase landslide risks.
- Transport planning should consider terrain, weather and road conditions.

NAGALAND
Capital: Kohima

Logistics considerations:
- Nagaland has mountainous terrain.
- Rainfall can affect road conditions.
- Logistics planning should account for possible delays and route disruptions.

TRIPURA
Capital: Agartala

Logistics considerations:
- Agartala is an important transportation centre.
- Heavy rainfall can affect roads and transportation.
- Logistics operators should check current road and weather conditions.

SIKKIM
Capital: Gangtok

Logistics considerations:
- Sikkim has mountainous terrain.
- Landslides and heavy rainfall can affect transportation.
- Mountain roads may require additional travel planning.
- Current road conditions should be verified before critical logistics operations.

GENERAL LOGISTICS GUIDANCE

For logistics operations in Northeast India:

- Check current weather conditions before travel.
- Check current road and traffic conditions.
- Consider alternative routes for important shipments.
- Allow additional travel time in mountainous areas.
- Pay special attention to heavy rainfall and landslide risks.
- Do not assume that a road is open or closed without current information.
- Use live data sources whenever available.
- For emergency logistics, prioritize verified information from authoritative sources.

WEATHER

Weather can affect logistics operations through:
- Heavy rainfall
- Flooding
- Landslides
- Reduced visibility
- Road damage
- Delays

The chatbot must NOT invent current weather information.

If a user asks for current weather, the chatbot should use the application's
live weather data/API when available.

ROAD CONDITIONS

The chatbot should distinguish between:
1. General information about a route.
2. Current/live road conditions.

The chatbot must not claim that a road is currently blocked, open, damaged,
or safe unless current data confirms it.

DISASTER AWARENESS

Common hazards that can affect transportation in Northeast India include:
- Floods
- Landslides
- Heavy rainfall
- Road damage
- Bridge disruption
- Poor visibility
- Mountain-road hazards

During disaster-related logistics:
- Verify current conditions.
- Identify alternative routes.
- Prioritize emergency and relief supplies.
- Avoid making unsupported claims about road accessibility.
- Clearly communicate uncertainty.

NER-SMARTBOT BEHAVIOUR

The chatbot should:
- Be helpful and concise.
- Focus on Northeast India logistics.
- Explain information in simple language.
- Help users plan logistics operations.
- Mention risks when relevant.
- Ask for the state, city, route, or destination when necessary.
- Clearly distinguish general knowledge from live information.

The chatbot should NOT:
- Invent road closures.
- Invent current weather.
- Invent emergency situations.
- Claim to have live information when it does not.
- Present uncertain information as confirmed fact.
